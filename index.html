How to play

  1. Set the table. Lay the phone flat in the middle. Everyone touches the screen once. That spot is yours.
  2. Tap whenever you like. No turns, no order. Each tap you land is a point.
  3. Watch the odds. The number in the middle is the chance the next tap busts. It starts tiny and climbs with every tap, at a different pace each round.
  4. Bank or bust. Bust and you get zero for the round and sit out the rest of it while everyone else keeps going.
  5. Bonus taps. Now and then a yellow tag drops in: the next tap is worth that many points instead of one. Same bust odds as any other tap.
  6. Quiet ends it. If nobody taps for 10 seconds, a 3-2-1 runs. Tap to keep going, or let it finish and everyone banks.
  7. Win. Most points after 10 rounds.

Settings can make a bust end the round for everyone instead.

Throw Hands

A second way to play. Pick it in settings.

  1. Check the bust line. Before each round the middle shows a number, like 12+. Every tap below it is safe. From 12 on, each tap might bust, and the odds get worse with every tap past it. It's never a sure thing.
  2. 3, 2, 1, fingers! Everyone throws one hand, 0 to 5 fingers, at once.
  3. Count up. Lowest hand goes first: tap your spot once per finger. Then the next lowest keeps the count going, and so on.
  4. Bust. If one of your taps busts, you get nothing this round. Same for everyone who hadn't gone yet.
  5. Score. Everyone who survives the round gets a point. Most points after 10 rounds wins.

Showed zero fingers? You don't tap. If you get marked as a bust by mistake, tap your name on the score sheet to flip it.
