How to play

  1. Set the table. Lay the phone flat in the middle. Everyone touches the screen once. That spot is yours.
  2. Check the bust line. Before each round the middle shows a number, like 12+. Every tap below it is safe. From 12 on, each tap might bust, and the odds get worse with every tap past it. It's never a sure thing.
  3. 3, 2, 1, fingers! Everyone throws one hand, 0 to 5 fingers, at once.
  4. Count up. Lowest hand goes first: tap your spot once per finger. Then the next lowest keeps the count going, and so on.
  5. Bust. If one of your taps busts, you get nothing this round. Same for everyone who hadn't gone yet.
  6. Score. Everyone who survives the round gets a point. Nobody ever sits out. Most points after 10 rounds wins.

Showed zero fingers? You don't tap. If you get marked out by mistake, tap your spot on the results screen to flip it.

Free for all

  1. No order, no hands. Anyone taps their spot whenever they like.
  2. Watch the odds. The middle shows the chance the next tap busts. It starts tiny and climbs with every tap, at a different pace each round.
  3. Bank or bust. Each tap you land is a point. Bust and you get zero for the round.
  4. Bonus taps. Now and then a yellow tag pops up: the next tap is worth extra. Same bust odds as any other tap.
  5. Quiet ends it. If nobody taps for 10 seconds, a 3-2-1 runs. Tap to keep going, or let it finish and everyone banks.
